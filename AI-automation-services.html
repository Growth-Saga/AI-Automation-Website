01AI agents

Agents that do the work, not just the talking.

The problem

Your best people spend hours on calls, replies and updates that follow the same pattern every time.

What we build

  • Voice agents that call, qualify on the call and book meetings straight into your calendar
  • Speed-to-lead agents that answer every new lead in under 60 seconds and route hot ones to the right rep
  • Voice-note agents: a salesperson talks after a meeting (in English, Hindi or Gujarati), and the AI writes the summary, rates the interest and sets the next step
  • Document agents that read business cards, CVs and forms in batches and fill in your records

Agents that close the loop, not just the ticket.

What changesFaster replies, more booked meetings, a CRM that's actually up to date, and hours back for your team.

Real example

For a printing-ink manufacturer, salespeople now speak a voice note after each visit. The AI turns it into a CRM entry with a summary, an interest rating and next steps.

See the case study →

Good fit if you lose leads to slow replies, or your team spends hours on calls and updates.

02AI automations

If a human shouldn't be doing it, a system should.

The problem

Your team copies data between systems, types in bills, chases payments and keeps processes alive in shared spreadsheets.

What we build

  • Invoice and bill entry: AI reads each bill, checks the maths and posts it to your accounting system
  • Payment reminders and alerts: sent on time, every time, without anyone chasing
  • Workflow apps that replace shared spreadsheets: purchase, production, order to dispatch, hiring. Every step has an owner and a due date
  • Live sync between your accounting system and your other tools, both ways, checked against the books
  • Tax and compliance checks that match every bill against the books

What changesHours of typing and chasing gone, fewer errors, and processes nobody has to babysit.

Real examples

  • Electrical project firm: a supplier bill is read, checked and posted straight to Tally, at ~$0.04 per invoice. The AI matches items correctly 97% of the time, learning from 1,216 past invoices.
  • Printing-ink manufacturer: 10 processes moved off shared Google Sheets into tracked apps, with 91 automatic alerts.
See the case studies →

Good fit if someone on your team spends hours a week typing, copying or chasing.

03AI dashboards

The number you need, the moment you need it.

The problem

You have an ERP, a CRM and ten spreadsheets, and still can't answer “how are we doing this week?” in under five minutes.

What we build

  • CEO dashboards: sales, cash, receivables and stock on one screen, live
  • Finance reports straight from your books: P&L, balance sheet, receivables, ageing, day book, stock
  • Site and cost-centre P&L: profit by project, branch or team
  • Plain-English AI summaries: “what changed this week and why”, written for you
  • Collections dashboards: who owes what, how late, and who's chasing it

What changesDecisions in minutes, not Monday meetings. Month-end reports are ready when you need them, not a week later.

Real examples

  • Printing-ink manufacturer: a 22-panel CEO dashboard that replaced a paid BI tool, matched to the last cent, plus ~35 live finance reports.
  • Electrical project firm: a year-long site profit report now runs in 30–40 seconds. It used to take about 7 minutes.
See the case studies →

Good fit if your numbers live in your accounting software, but nobody can see them without a spreadsheet.

04AI GTM for B2B & SaaS

Pipeline you can forecast, not hope for.

The problem

Strong delivery, no steady pipeline. The founder does the prospecting, and generic outreach gets weak replies.

What we build

  • Ideal-customer research: who buys from you, and why
  • List building and enrichment: the right companies and the right people, with verified contact details
  • Signal-based targeting: reach out when they're hiring, raising or growing
  • Personal outreach at scale: AI-written emails that sound like you
  • Booking and routing: replies are sorted and meetings land in the right calendar

What changesA steady flow of meetings, and every stage measured, so you know your cost per meeting and where deals stall.

Real example

For a cost-optimisation consultancy in the UAE, we built the full engine: find, enrich, personal outreach and AI booking straight to the calendar.

Good fit if you sell B2B, your deals are worth chasing, and you need more meetings without hiring more SDRs.
